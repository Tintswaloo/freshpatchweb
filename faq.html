Frequently Asked Questions

📦 How long does delivery take?

We deliver within 1–2 days in Gauteng. For other areas, 2–4 working days.

💳 What payment options are available?

We currently support EFT, cash on delivery, and mobile payments (SnapScan).

🔁 Do you offer refunds or exchanges?

Yes, refunds or replacements are offered within 48 hours for perishable goods.

🌿 Where do your products come from?

We work directly with trusted local farms in South Africa.

🧺 Can I customize my Fresh Box?

Not right now, but we’re working on letting you swap items in the future :)
